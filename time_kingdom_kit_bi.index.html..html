💡 Primary Math Time Phrasing Rules

📌 Word Format: Expressed as "2 o'clock", "Quarter past 2", "Half past 2", "Ten to 3";
📌 Digital Format: Expressed as "2:00", "3:07" (separated by colon, leading zero for single-digit minutes like 3:07).

📝 Digital Format ➔ Word Format

2:00 ➔ 2 o'clock
3:07 ➔ 7 minutes past 3
2:15 ➔ Quarter past 2
2:30 ➔ Half past 2

🔢 Word Format ➔ Digital Format

2 o'clock ➔ 2:00
7 minutes past 3 ➔ 3:07
Five past 5 ➔ 5:05
Quarter to 11 ➔ 10:45
